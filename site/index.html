Adding, removing or renaming issues

  1. Name the PDF YYYY-MM-DD Title.pdf (or YYYY-MM Title.pdf), e.g. 2026-10 Autumn Newsletter.pdf. The date controls the order; the newest is shown first.
  2. Go to Upload files to the pdfs folder, drag the PDF in and press Commit changes.
  3. Wait about a minute — the site rebuilds automatically and both embeds update.
  4. To remove or rename an issue, open the pdfs folder, click the file, then use the … menu to delete it, or the pencil icon to rename it.
